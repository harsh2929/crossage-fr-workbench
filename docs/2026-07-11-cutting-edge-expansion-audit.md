## Executive summary

The 2026 audit identified four frontiers where Vintrace was behind. Implementation has since closed the generative/conversational, model-native intelligence, and RAW/pro/open-portability gaps. ## Platform 1 — Recognition / ML backend

**Convergent thesis (internal audit + external SOTA agree):** the recognition *decision math* is already near its constrained ceiling. The 2019-era ArcFace/IResNet backbone is behind 2024–25 ViT recognizers (LVFace ranks #1 on MFR-Ongoing, ~97.25% TAR@FAR=1e-5 on IJB-C), **but a backbone swap hits a hard licensing trap**: nearly every high-accuracy open face model (LVFace, buffalo/antelopev2, most AdaFace weights) is **non-commercial via its training data** (MS1M/Glint360K/WebFace). ## Risks & watch-items

- **The weights-licensing trap.** Nearly every high-accuracy face model and several VLMs (LVFace, buffalo/antelopev2, FLUX dev, Apple FastVLM, Jina-v4) are **non-commercial via training data or license**. Every model recommendation above is chosen for a permissive license (Apache/MIT/permissive) *or* flagged ⚠. Vet licenses per-model before shipping; the safe generative/embedding/VLM stack is SigLIP 2 / Perception Encoder / Qwen3-VL / Qwen-Image-Edit / PaddleOCR / LaMa / Real-ESRGAN.
- **The regulatory clock.** Article 50 applicability remains role/use specific. The Council's 29 June 2026 final-adoption notice reports a **2 Dec 2026** transition deadline for affected generated-content transparency measures and later high-risk dates. Workspace-local signed C2PA credentials, consent/retention, and disclosure UX are implemented, but they do not decide legal applicability or replace current legal review and real policy publication.
- **Prompt injection** remains a high-value regression surface as the app ingests more connector/OCR/web text. The typed isolation and answer re-entry boundary is implemented; preserve its protocol and adversarial tests as new ingestion paths land.
- **Distribution trust.** Default-unsigned builds undercut a privacy product's credibility. Sign/notarize before broad release.
- **Scope discipline.** On-device generative models are large; gate the heavy tiers (Qwen-Image-Edit ~20B) behind hardware capability detection and keep light permissive defaults (LaMa/Real-ESRGAN) for low-end machines. Watch energy/thermal cost.
